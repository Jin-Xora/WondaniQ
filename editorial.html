Editorial principles

How Wondaniq approaches research, explanations and commercial transparency.

Explain before recommending

Our primary purpose is to explain technologies and concepts. Product mentions should provide relevant context, not disguised endorsements.

Accuracy and clarity

We aim to distinguish established facts, examples and interpretation, use accessible language and update material when important information changes.

Commercial transparency

Affiliate links, sponsorships and other commercial relationships should be clearly disclosed. Commercial relationships should not determine editorial conclusions.
